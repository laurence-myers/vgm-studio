How they compare

P1 is the loudest “on” and the purest backlit-pad idea. P2 has the most character and the best idle discoverability — buttons read as buttons before you hover — at the cost of a brighter, busier deck. P3 is the quietest and the easiest migration from the current dark look. P4 keeps the calm deck of P3 but gives latched state a stronger, shaped signal. All four use identical geometry, so they differ only in palette-style parameters — the ButtonChrome refactor covers any of them, and switching between them later is just numbers.

Implementation notes. Without outer halos, every effect fits inside the widget rect: rounded fill, 1px border, an inset highlight/shadow line, and (P1/P2) a lit cap — two or three concentric fills stand in for the radial gradient at this size, or a flat lit colour honestly reads fine at 26px. P3's legend bloom is the icon drawn twice (once fat and translucent, once solid). No background-layer tricks, no neighbour clipping. The ButtonChrome::Pad branch from the earlier estimate shrinks by the glow code: all four variants are the same ~60–80 line painter with different palette numbers.
