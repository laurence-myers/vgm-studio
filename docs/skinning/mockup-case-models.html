How to read the three

Scan the Cream column top to bottom — it is the case where the models differ most: dark deck + cream keys (A), a fully light plate (B), or the dark deck kept on purpose (C). Then compare Navy in A vs B: keys-only versus the whole fascia. B and C are identical for dark and mid cases; they part ways only on the light ones. Geometry, icons, wells and the amber lamp never move — the choice is purely which surfaces the case may paint, so switching between these models later is cheap.

Implementation note. All three reduce to the same code: a case supplies key colours (A), plate colours (B), or both plus a deck/plate flag (C). The pad painter and plate painter already exist on the rust branch; the model picked here decides what the twelve CaseColors tables contain, not how they are painted.
